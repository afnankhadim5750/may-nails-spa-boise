Nails

Classic manicures

The foundation of it all. Nails are shaped to flatter your hands, cuticles are gently cared for, and polish is applied in smooth, even coats for a clean, lasting finish.

  • Shaping tailored to your nail beds and lifestyle
  • Gentle cuticle care and nail conditioning

Spa

Spa pedicures

An hour of pure unwind. Feet soak in warm, fresh water before exfoliation, a relaxing massage and careful nail care — finished with the polish of your choice.

  • Warm soak with fresh water for every guest
  • Exfoliation and callus smoothing
  • Massage and hot-towel finish

Hand-painted nail art

From a single delicate accent to a full set of tiny artworks. Our artists paint freehand — florals, French twists, minimal linework, seasonal motifs and more.

  • Accent nails or full-set artwork

Acrylics & gel

Length, strength and shine that goes the distance. Extensions and overlays are sculpted and shaped — almond, coffin, square or oval — then finished to a glossy, durable shine.
