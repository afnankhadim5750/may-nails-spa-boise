Hygiene & care

Clean tools. Calm mind.

Great nails start with a spotless station. Our sanitation routine is built around a few simple, non-negotiable standards:

  • Disinfected, every time

    Metal implements are cleaned and disinfected between every guest, without exception.

  • Single-use essentials

    Files, buffers and other porous tools are used once and replaced — never shared.

  • Fresh soak water

    Pedicure basins are drained, scrubbed and refilled with fresh water for each appointment.

  • Stations reset between guests

    Every table is wiped down and restocked before your service begins.
